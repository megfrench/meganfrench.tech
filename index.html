SHOP FLOOR TO FEDERAL CONTRACTOR: MY ACADEMIC & PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY

Greetings, colleagues, fellow contractors, and lurkers...

For the past several years, I have been balancing the hands on and extremely high stakes environment of infiltrating and integrating myself into different roles. My current mission is operating presses at EPC here in Southern Ohio with the rigorous academic demands of my Bachelor's degree program at University of the People. It is a challenging world of raw materials, heavy machinery, and strict deadlines, but it forms the backbone of American industry.

I am eagerly preparing to resume my studies this October after a short LOA to source the perfect manufacturing day job for myself.

"Two things are infinite: the universe and human stupidity; and I am not sure about the universe."
- Albert Einstein

My current career trajectory serves a specific purpose: to master the intricacies of federal procurement. Working the presses at EPC has given me invaluable ground level experience in manufacturing, quality control, and supply chain realities. You cannot truly comprehend defense contracting without understanding the factory floor.

Through this digital log, I track my professional development, coursework, and the insights I gain bridging the gap between the grit of Southern Ohio manufacturing and the glam of Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR). Practical experience and academic theory are not separate studies in my opinion. They help make sense of one another to create a fully realized understanding of a full end to end system.
