Facility & Equipment

Our Piketon, OH facility is optimized for continuous, high-yield production. We maintain a versatile fleet of modern injection molding presses to accommodate rapid prototyping through high-volume commercial runs.

  • Heavy Press Capacity: Six (6) state-of-the-art injection molding presses ranging from 125 to 400 tons.
  • Continuous Operation: 24/7 facility capabilities built to handle immediate capacity backfill and overflow runs.
  • Integrated Automation: Advanced robotics and conveyor systems for efficient part removal and high-speed cycle times.

Quality & Compliance

We strictly adhere to industry-leading quality management systems, ensuring every component meets exact dimensional tolerances and material specifications.

  • ISO 9001 Certified: Standardized, repeatable, and auditable production workflows.
  • 100% Domestic Resin Sourcing: Fully compliant with Buy American Act (BAA) and NDAA material standards.
  • ITAR Registration Pathway: Active protocols in place for handling defense-related technical data securely.
